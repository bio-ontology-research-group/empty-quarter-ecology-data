# DNA extraction from soil samples

**Source:** [manufacturer protocol link](https://www.qiagen.com/us/resources/download.aspx?id=9bb59b74-e493-4aeb-b6c1-f660852e8d97&lang=en)

**Notes for this project:**

- 0.9 to 1.2 g of soil were used as input.
- Samples were homogenized with the **PowerLyzer 24 Homogenizer** (QIAGEN, lot 154049400).  
- Downstream extraction steps were performed either manually or using the **QIACube Connect** (QIAGEN, #9002864).  
- DNA was eluted in **50 µL** of elution buffer.
