Scientific Data has no main-section word limits. Data Records describes
repositories, files, formats and fields; filenames are permitted there.
Technical validation belongs in its own section. An abstract of up to 170
words is recommended and titles are limited to 110 characters. Author roles
are required in SNAPP; its instructions say to omit that section from the
article. The older MTS system requires it in the article. Data must be
accessible to reviewers at submission. Related manuscripts must be disclosed.
ISME additionally requests copies and an explanation of the distinction in
its cover letter. These requirements do not constitute an acceptance decision.
