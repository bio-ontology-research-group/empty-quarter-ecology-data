Venue rationale
Scientific Data discourages separate supplementary text and recommends
including it in the article wherever practical. Its allowance for substantial
additional length made the former 12-page supplement permissible, but did
not require it. Main-article tables must fit one printed page; the complete
93-row chemical mapping is therefore linked as repository data rather than
reproduced as a multi-page table.
https://www.nature.com/sdata/submission-guidelines
