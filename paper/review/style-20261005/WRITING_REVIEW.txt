Assessment

The paper has a defensible central argument: repeated sampling needs links
between site visits, physical specimens, processing steps and measurements;
the graph preserves those links for retrieval and reuse. The introduction
establishes that problem, and the measurement example explains the quality /
value distinction well. The current prose generally avoids inflated novelty,
paragraph-opening bold labels, em dashes and unsupported causal claims.
Keep the Scientific Data section order, Rund's experimental descriptions,
scientific qualifications, and the existing figures and formal examples.

The main weakness is competing levels of explanation. Scientific methods,
source-repair history, deployment instructions and repeated validation
inventories frequently occupy the same paragraph. This makes the paper read
like a release audit. Conservative editing should restore a direct account
of the resource while keeping the detailed provenance in the cited ledgers.
Do not turn historical v3.0.0 checks into claims of new v3.0.1 validation.

Proposed corrections, in priority order

1. Repair the laboratory XRF sentence and acquisition cross-reference.
   02_methods.tex, XRF acquisition, processing and reconciliation.
   The text currently says "The laboratory exports leave ... remain
   incomplete", which has two competing predicates. Replace with:

   "The laboratory exports lack the exact model variant, serial number,
   measurement duration, tube settings, sample preparation, concentration
   units and calibration records."

   In the preceding paragraph, replace "Supplementary Section S2" with
   "Suppl. Section S3": XRF acquisition evidence is S3; S2 covers library
   preparation and pH. Retain S2 for the pooling calculation elsewhere.
   In supplement.tex, distinguish confirmed Vanta family from the missing
   exact model: add "Laboratory measurements used a Vanta handheld analyser."
   and replace "laboratory instrument identity" with "exact laboratory
   model variant and serial number" in the list of missing workbook fields.
   This uses Robert's 4 October confirmation, without inventing settings.

2. Make the shotgun subsection unambiguously about the available data.
   02_methods.tex, Shotgun-derived companion inputs.
   "Companion" is used for inputs, comparisons and encoded function without
   consistently identifying the ecology analysis. After the correction to
   the ecology paper, it should not suggest a separate shotgun publication.
   Rename the subsection "Shotgun-derived data". Open with:

   "We distribute genome-relative-abundance profiles for 150 shotgun
   libraries and eggNOG-mapper annotations of the genome catalogue for
   comparison with amplicon-derived functional profiles in the ecology
   analysis."

   Keep the CoverM and annotation paths, versions, 991/990 catalogue counts,
   975 marker subset, and 125/119 matching denominators. Replace "companion
   comparison" with "ecology analysis" and delete the final sentence that
   repeats the purpose. End with the existing concrete requirements for
   reproduction from raw reads. These requirements must remain visible.

3. State the pH protocol and matching level without recounting the repair.
   02_methods.tex, Archived-soil pH acquisition and admission.
   Preserve workbook equivalence and release IDs where needed to connect
   the two papers, but move the account of 155 repaired links and 620 RDF
   relation changes to the reconciliation ledger. Replace the paragraph
   beginning "All Trip 4 pH assays" with:

   "All 156 admitted Trip 4 pH measurements used archived field replicate 2.
   The versioned specimen ledger preserves the original workbook identifiers
   and links each admitted measurement to its physical specimen. Matching
   with community profiles is at campaign, site and compartment level.
   The archived RDF protocol identifies an accumet AB315 with Probe 3 and
   automatic temperature compensation, and specifies mixing without fine
   grinding."

   The 156 is already in the coverage table (155 reconciled plus one
   previously linked specimen). No measurement or analytical result changes.

4. Keep current climate provenance; remove revision language.
   02_methods.tex, Environmental and climate data.
   Retain the exact coordinate, acquisition dates, request/response evidence,
   product distinctions and frozen monthly KG provenance. Use "versioned
   daily-series package" for "versioned replacement package" and name
   Site 52's grid cells directly instead of repeatedly calling them
   "corrected". The machine-readable paths retain their exact names.
   Describe which inputs each analysis uses; put the old-position comparison
   in the package provenance. Do not imply that the monthly KG observations
   were regenerated from the daily acquisition.

5. Remove duplicated extraction prose and an editing-history phrase.
   02_methods.tex, DNA extraction and sequencing.
   The extract-concentration paragraph says twice that the spreadsheet
   retains concentration measurements. Suggested wording:

   "We quantified extracted DNA using the Qubit dsDNA High Sensitivity assay,
   with fluorescence measured on a Varioskan LUX multimode plate reader
   (Thermo Fisher Scientific). We recorded extract concentrations and
   processing information in the central project spreadsheet. Progression
   to indexing and sequencing required an amplicon of the expected size
   after first-round PCR. Extracted DNA was stored at -20 degrees C until
   amplification."

   Later, replace "the earlier laboratory account also documents
   re-extraction" with "the laboratory protocol also included
   re-extraction". Retain the documented scope; do not imply every failed
   sample was re-extracted.

6. Split the long Data Records inventory by scientific role.
   04_data_records.tex, Tabular data products.
   Its long opening sentence mixes source ledgers, identity repairs, assay
   tables and downstream analytical inputs. Use three connected paragraphs:
   (a) the source ledger links specimens to extraction, libraries and runs;
   (b) assay tables carry taxonomic, environmental, XRF and pH observations;
   (c) PMA and shotgun-derived files support the ecology analyses.
   Keep every count, file path and denominator. The existing final paragraph
   can continue to identify the upstream resources required for full replay.
   This is paragraph-level editing within the current subsection.

7. Shorten repeated query and validation descriptions.
   05_validation.tex, Competency questions and SPARQL queries.
   Explain the Site 10 query once, immediately beside its listing: it traverses
   site -> measurement process -> output value -> analyte quality, returning
   46 rows (24 and 22 from the two named processes). The surrounding paragraphs
   repeat the count, archive location and validation requirement. Consolidate
   those statements and distinguish the cardinality test from the source-value
   comparison. Preserve all result counts and exact tolerances.
   Keep the dated v3.0.0 acceptance results and the separate v3.0.1 evidence;
   their version distinction is scientific provenance, not expendable history.

8. Reduce implementation detail in the finite-rule description.
   03_knowledge_representation.tex, OWL and RDF implementation.
   Keep the rule scope, use of existing RDF terms, fixed-point stopping rule,
   failure conditions and clash diagnostics. The class-partition inventory
   refresh and per-batch controller trace can be described in linked workflow
   documentation. Begin with the operative distinction:

   "The public KG v3.0.1 service queries the asserted union of the distributed
   modules. A separate finite-rule workflow derives additional consequences
   over existing RDF terms."

   This explains the reader-visible behavior before the algorithm details.
   Do not claim a completed reasoned release or unrestricted OWL reasoning.

9. Replace software-audit jargon in prose with the check performed.
   05_validation.tex: "site-alias gate", "environmental-metadata gate",
   "chemical-identifier gate" and "label gate" should be "check" or
   "validation", followed by the concrete operation. Keep exact code and
   artifact names in paths. 04_data_records.tex's "outstanding
   sequence-release gate" can be "raw-sequence availability".
   These changes make the account accessible without weakening requirements.

10. Trim metacommentary and redundant inventory text.
    kr_supplement.tex, laboratory-control example: delete "This keeps long
    machine identifiers in the executable resource while exposing the
    scientific relationships here." It explains a writing choice rather
    than the model. Keep the paths and the subject/relation/taxon example.
    05_validation.tex, Portal access: delete "This is an access interface."
    The preceding sentence already explains its function.
    The introduction's resource-count paragraph duplicates the abstract and
    coverage tables. A later shortening pass could retain the primary sites,
    campaigns and specimen/profile distinction while referring to the tables
    for assay counts. This is optional, not necessary to correct the paper.

Material to preserve

- The main-section order, authors, figure content and formal definitions.
- All scientific values, units, inclusion criteria, counts and identifiers.
- Explicit limitations where records are missing; do not remove them merely
  because a positive statement of scope would be shorter.
- The declared scopes of ShEx, the full taxonomy parser, ELK and finite rules.
- Historical validation versions and the distinction between scientific
  input regeneration, module loading and raw-read reconstruction.
- Exact software versions and operational paths needed to reproduce analyses.
