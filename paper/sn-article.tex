\abstract{Environmental microbiome reuse requires field, molecular and laboratory data to remain connected to provenance. We describe a multimodal resource from five expeditions across a 1,043~km cumulative path (1,015~km end to end) in the Rub' al-Khali from March 2023 to October 2025. A 2,550-row source ledger connects specimens, DNA extractions, libraries, runs and quality-controlled community profiles; 2,516 non-control records are sample individuals in an SIO-based OWL graph. The canonical feature table contains 1,271 profiles; 1,237 pass ecological filtering across 64 numeric site labels. Additional records comprise 274 environmental logs, 12,936 monthly climate measurements, 712 admitted archived-soil pH observations, and two uncalibrated X-ray fluorescence workflows (71 field sessions at 58 sites and 725 laboratory records). Shape Expressions, bounded consistency checks and an executable query validate stated structural coverage. The package supports provenance-aware reuse, not an operational digital twin.}
