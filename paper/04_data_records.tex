\section{Data records}\label{records}

The source and derived-data package contains graph modules, curated
tables, specimen and assay provenance, validation evidence and workflow
code. The machine-readable manifest
\path{PRE_RELEASE_MANIFEST.tsv} identifies payload sizes, checksums,
categories and release/licence status. Separately retrieved bulk
payloads are listed in the bulk manifest. Data Availability reports
access routes and raw-sequence availability.

KG v3.0.1 contains 45,707,660 asserted triples from 16 manifested
modules and is archived at Zenodo \cite{rubalkhali_kg301}.
The release distributes the original RDF modules, the deduplicated
asserted graph in compressed N-Quads format, and a rebuild kit containing
load/export code, definition provenance and validation evidence.
The \href{https://bio2vec.net/data/empty-quarter/kg/3.0.1/manifest.json}{release manifest}
identifies their contents, sizes and checksums. The kit reconstructs the
asserted union from the distributed modules. Scientific producer code and
inputs are maintained separately in the reproducibility repository.

\subsection{Knowledge-graph modules}

The primary artifacts are the knowledge-graph modules, distributed as
RDF/XML and Turtle files and structured by data type
(Table~\ref{tab:modules}). The base ontology
(\texttt{rubalkhali.owl}) holds the project terminology. Domain modules
share site, specimen and process identifiers; the release also includes
the generated taxonomic vocabulary and four pinned reference ontologies.
These modules load together into an RDF store. Domain queries require the corresponding
shared terminology and linked site, specimen or process modules.

\subsection{Tabular data products}

The specimen catalogue links site and campaign to specimen, extraction,
library, sequence run, quality-control decision and derived community.
Identifiers 61--64 denote four named sites sampled only in Trip~1 and
account for 36 source specimens. Assay tables provide the canonical amplicon
profiles, taxonomic mappings, field environmental observations, laboratory
and field XRF measurements, and archived-soil pH values.

The canonical feature table contains 1,271 profiles. The ecology analysis
retains 1,237 (1,227 profiles from sites~1--60), after excluding 24 controls and ten
biological profiles with fewer than 1,000 reads, including one
\texttt{T1Dr1} run with 934 reads. The pH release
\texttt{EQ-PH-SHARED-v1.0.1} provides 712 measurements and their
specimen and session links.

Four additional products support downstream ecology analyses: the paired
PMA table (\path{metadata/relic-dna/PMA_ASV_table.tsv}; 18 analytical
columns from nine treated/untreated pairs, with controls kept separately),
150 CoverM profiles (\path{metadata/metagenome/coverm_profiles.tar.gz}),
eggNOG annotations (\path{metadata/metagenome/eq.emapper.annotations.gz})
for the 990-genome analytical subset, and six source tables for the
MAG-derived versus PICRUSt2 comparison
(\path{metadata/metagenome/measured_function_inputs.tar.gz}).
The daily climate inputs are selected by
\path{metadata/climate/current_analysis_inputs.json}; the monthly acquisition
is documented in \path{metadata/climate/climate_acquisition_frozen.json}.
The complete field environmental observations are provided in
\path{metadata/environmental/environmental_measurements_curated.tsv}.
The sequence inventory and functional-prediction provenance are distributed
under \path{metadata/amplicon/} and \path{metadata/functional/}.
Methods identifies the upstream resources required for reproduction from
raw reads.

\subsection{Validation schemas, queries and evidence}

The reproducibility repository includes the artifacts needed to re-validate the resource:
the ShEx shapes that define the structural contract for each entity
family, the positive and negative structural fixtures, the archived
competency SPARQL queries, and the machine-readable evidence bundles
(reconciliation summaries, audit tables, validation records and
checksum manifests) produced by the release workflow. A companion data
dictionary (\path{metadata/DATA_DICTIONARY.tsv}) records the field
names, data types, units, missing-value conventions and definitions
for the curated canonical tabular records. Quantities carry units only
where a unit is documented at the source: environmental source tables
retain degrees Celsius, hectopascals (millibars) and percent relative
humidity; in the graph, pressure magnitudes are
multiplied by 100 and asserted in pascals (\texttt{obo:UO\_0000110}),
climate
records use degrees Celsius and millimetres, and DNA concentrations
are in nanograms per microlitre. XRF values are instrument-reported magnitudes with undocumented
concentration units.

The manifests and data dictionary provide the versioned file-level
inventory. The KG citation identifies the immutable v3.0.1 deposit
(Section~\ref{data_availability}); the repository manifests additionally
cover the broader analytical inputs.
