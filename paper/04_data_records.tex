\section{Data records}\label{records}

KG v3.0.1 contains 45,707,660 asserted triples from 16
modules and is archived at Zenodo \cite{rubalkhali_kg301}. The resource contains the modules listed in Table~\ref{tab:modules},
the combined asserted graph, and a reproducibility package with code,
term definitions and validation evidence. The modules use RDF/XML and
Turtle, and the combined graph uses compressed N-Quads. Shared site, sample and
process identifiers connect observations across modules. The deposit also includes an inventory with checksums.

Table~\ref{tab:products} distinguishes the biological products from their graph metadata.
The sample catalogue links collection visits, specimens, extractions, libraries and runs;
these entities retain separate identifiers because a specimen can yield several assays or runs.
The data dictionary specifies types, units and missing-value conventions; the climate inventory
records acquisition windows and variables; and the file inventory records sizes, checksums,
licensing and retrieval information. Archived-soil pH measurements and their Trip~4 replicate-2
specimen reconciliation are distributed under version identifier \texttt{EQ-PH-SHARED-v1.0.1}.
A machine-readable cohort and product manifest assigns manuscript and reproducibility snapshot
identifiers to distinguish the canonical released resource from companion analytical products
and historical pilots until formal repository deposition. These snapshot identifiers link the
canonical 5,438-representative genome catalogue, the separate Bakta
(\texttt{EQ-PROT-BAKTA-v1.0.0}) and Pyrodigal (\texttt{EQ-PROT-PYRODIGAL-REP-v1.0.0})
gene-calling products, and the four CoverM read-mapping products
(\texttt{EQ-COVERM-REFINED-v1.0.0}, \texttt{EQ-COVERM-REL-ABUND-v1.0.0},
\texttt{EQ-COVERM-ARCHAEA-COMP-v1.0.0} and \texttt{EQ-COVERM-LEGACY-991-v0}) to the exact
specimen and library cohorts reused by the companion studies
\cite{ecology_companion,proteome_companion}. The paired PMA profiles and the historical
Trips~1--3 991-genome pilot analysis (150 CoverM profiles, a 990-genome KO matrix and
six source tables for the initial MAG-derived versus PICRUSt2 comparison) retain their own
reference definitions and analysis provenance.
The microTrait trait profile (\texttt{EQ-MICROTRAIT-\allowbreak REP-\allowbreak v1.0.0}) provides genome-resolved trait matrices and continuous predictions as a separate annotation product, with compact tables and provenance in the reproducibility package and larger intermediates retained on institutional storage. This annotation product is separate from the archived KG v3.0.1; its bulk deposit is included in the pending archive described in Data Availability.

\subsection{FAIR identifiers and reuse metadata}
The version-specific Zenodo DOI, ENA accessions and project-domain IRIs support discovery and identification of the resource~\cite{fair}. The archived modules provide fixed inputs for local access, while the public endpoint provides query access to the deployed asserted graph. The release manifest identifies files by checksum and the data dictionary defines fields, units and missing-value conventions. Data Availability records the access conditions for sequences and associated products.

Interoperability is supported by RDF and shared SIO, ENVO, ChEBI, PATO, UO and validated taxonomic identifiers. Original labels and classifier lineages remain linked to their mapped identifiers, preserving the evidence used to interpret mappings. Specimen, process and measurement provenance, term definitions, validation evidence and scoped licences support reuse. Imported vocabularies retain their own licences; project-authored metadata and software have the licences stated in Data Availability.
