\section{Library preparation and archived-soil pH}
\label{supp:laboratory_protocol}

First-round PCR used 50~\textmu L reactions with 1$\times$
Mg$^{2+}$-containing buffer, 0.2~mM of each dNTP,
0.4~\textmu M of each primer and 1.25~U of TaKaRa Ex Taq.
The programme comprised 95\degree C for 3~min, followed by
35 cycles of 95\degree C for 10~s, 65\degree C for 30~s and
72\degree C for 1~min, with a final extension at 75\degree C
for 5~min. Initial DNA input was 5~\textmu L; repeat attempts
used 8 and 12~\textmu L while retaining the 50~\textmu L total
reaction volume. Indexed libraries underwent 0.8:1 AMPure XP cleanup.
The retained indexing protocol specifies the kit and thermal
programme; its reaction-specific reagent volumes remain undocumented.

Pooling used library molarity derived from measured DNA concentration
and fragment length. Aliquot volumes were calculated using a pooling-volume factor of the
form $VC/N$, where $V$ is target pool volume, $C$ is target molarity
and $N$ is the number of libraries; dividing this factor by a library's
molarity gives that library's aliquot volume. Run-specific target volumes, target molarities and individual library
measurements remain undocumented.

Both PMA-treated and untreated aliquots underwent 10~min in darkness
and 35~min of illumination at 450~nm. Each of three parent collection
tubes supplied three separately prepared slurries, with each slurry
split into a treated and an untreated aliquot.

Archived-soil pH suspensions were mixed for 30~min on a HulaMixer,
then stood for 30~min at room temperature. Measurements used an
accumet AB315 meter and Fisher Scientific accumet electrode
13-620-112, with the green stability indicator as the reading endpoint.
All Trip~4 assays used archived field replicate~2. Each admitted measurement is linked to its physical specimen and
retains its original assay identifier and value.

\section{XRF acquisition evidence}\label{supp:xrf_acquisition}

Laboratory XRF used a Vanta handheld analyser in Fast Mode for 547
specimens from Trips~1--4 and 178 from Trip~5. The Trip~5 acquisition
settings were Fast Screening-He8mm, helium, an 8~mm diameter and oxide
reporting. These specimens comprise 60 shallow-subsurface, 60 surface
and 58 root-adjacent samples. For V4Dr3 and V18Dr3, the analytical
dataset uses Fast Screening measurements; additional Best Detection
measurements are retained as acquisition data.

The exact method label, gas and diameter for Trips~1--4 remain
undocumented. Handheld field measurements used Geochem(3-Beam) and
NORMAL settings on an Olympus Vanta analyser; acquisition duration
was unrecorded.

The concentration observations retain their instrument-reported magnitudes.
Percentages associated with statistical error and units attached to
detection limits describe those quantities. Concentration units, the exact
laboratory model variant and serial number, exposure settings, sample
preparation and calibration results remain undocumented. Acquisition
validation checks specimen selection, repeated observations and the
unit evidence described in the main article.
