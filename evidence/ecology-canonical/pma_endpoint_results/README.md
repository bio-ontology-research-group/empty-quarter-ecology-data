# Paired Trip-5 PMA endpoints

In nine paired Trip-5 aliquots, PMA treatment reduced expected rarefied richness, while no Shannon-diversity difference was detected.

- Pairs: 9
- Exact expected-richness depth: 123,897 reads
- Expected rarefied richness, treated versus untreated mean: 608.5 versus 771.8
- Mean treated-minus-untreated richness difference, paired-bootstrap 95% interval: -163.3 [-274.0, -69.8]
- Richness paired exact Wilcoxon: W=1, p=0.0078125
- Shannon, treated versus untreated mean: 4.32 versus 4.19
- Mean treated-minus-untreated Shannon difference, paired-bootstrap 95% interval: 0.13 [-0.15, 0.47]
- Shannon paired exact Wilcoxon: W=18, p=0.652344

The richness endpoint is an exact expectation, not a random rarefaction. The percentile intervals resample the nine matched aliquot pairs and use the recorded seed. A nonsignificant Shannon test is reported as no detected difference, not as equivalence.

These endpoints do not quantify a relic-DNA fraction, establish cell viability, or support a survey-wide mechanism.
