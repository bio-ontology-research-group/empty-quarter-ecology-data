- Status: `bounded_elemental_axis_association`
- Cohort: 621 laboratory-XRF/community joins over 60 core sites
- Primary partial R2: 0.00358 (site-cluster resampling median 0.00543 over 38.1 distinct sites on average; upward biased, so no interval is reported)
- Primary within-site permutation p: 0.01
- Residual multivariate Moran I: 0.3042 (p = 0.001)
- Every taxon-set/zero-treatment fit supported: True
- Every partial R2 below 0.01: True

## Permitted wording

In Aitchison geometry the laboratory-XRF elemental axis remained associated with community composition after campaign, position and site adjustment in every prespecified taxon set and zero treatment (primary partial R2 = 0.0036, p = 0.01). The axis accounts for well under one percent of adjusted compositional variation, so report it as a bounded association with an elemental axis only. Do not infer salinity, soluble ions, osmotic stress or causality. Composition remained spatially autocorrelated after campaign, position and the elemental axis (site-level multivariate Moran I = 0.304, p = 0.001), so the axis is not a spatially controlled explanation and geography remains an unmodelled alternative.

## Prohibited wording

Do not call the axis salinity, and do not infer soluble ions, osmotic stress, or causality; no EC, pH or soluble-ion measurement supports that reading. Field XRF is not pooled with laboratory XRF.
