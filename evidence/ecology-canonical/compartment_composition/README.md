## Permitted wording

Paired compositional comparisons with the site as the permutation unit supported a multivariate location difference for shallow subsurface versus surface; root-adjacent versus surface; root-adjacent versus shallow subsurface, and the direction and location result persisted across taxon-set, zero-treatment and leave-one-campaign fits. Within-position dispersion also differed for shallow subsurface versus surface; root-adjacent versus surface; root-adjacent versus shallow subsurface, so the compartments differ in spread as well as in location; the location result itself is a paired sign-flip test of the mean within-block difference vector and is exact under the paired-symmetry null irrespective of that dispersion heterogeneity. A retained compartment effect describes composition only. Plant identity, root activity, root distance and local moisture were not measured, so it must not be interpreted as a plant selective filter.

## Prohibited wording

Do not describe any compartment difference as a plant selective filter, rhizosphere effect mechanism, or moisture-driven selection; none of those variables were measured.
